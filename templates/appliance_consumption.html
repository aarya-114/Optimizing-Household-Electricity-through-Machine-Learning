Energy Saving Tips

  • Set your AC to 24-26°C instead of lower temperatures
  • Use programmable thermostats to reduce cooling when not at home
  • Clean AC filters regularly for optimal efficiency
  • Consider using ceiling fans along with AC to distribute cool air better

  • Keep your refrigerator temperature between 3-5°C for optimal efficiency
  • Ensure good air circulation around the refrigerator
  • Clean the coils regularly to maintain efficiency
  • Check door seals to prevent cold air leakage

  • Use microwave instead of oven when possible
  • Match pot size to burner size on the stove
  • Use lids when cooking to retain heat and cook faster
  • Run dishwashers only when full and use eco mode if available
